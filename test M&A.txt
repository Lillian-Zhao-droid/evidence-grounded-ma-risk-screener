Item 2.01 – Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets

On October 11, 2022, Parent completed the Acquisition. At the effective time of the Acquisition, all outstanding Company Ordinary Shares were acquired by Purchaser from Nielsen’s shareholders in accordance with the provisions of the Scheme, in consideration of a payment of $28.00 in cash for each Company Ordinary Share.

Pursuant to the Transaction Agreement, each outstanding option to purchase Company Ordinary Shares was cancelled and converted into the right to receive a cash payment based on the excess of the Offer Consideration over the applicable exercise price.

At the Effective Time, each outstanding restricted stock unit award in respect of Company Ordinary Shares was cancelled and converted into the right to receive a cash payment equal to the number of Company Ordinary Shares subject to such award multiplied by the Offer Consideration.

The description of the Acquisition and the Transaction Agreement contained in this Item 2.01 is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Transaction Agreement previously filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

Item 3.01 – Notice of Delisting or Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard; Transfer of Listing

Prior to the Effective Time, Company Ordinary Shares were listed and traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the trading symbol “NLSN.”

On October 11, 2022, Nielsen notified the NYSE that the Acquisition had been completed and requested suspension of trading of Company Ordinary Shares prior to the opening of trading on October 12, 2022.

Nielsen also requested that the NYSE file a notification of removal from listing and registration on Form 25 to effect the delisting of all Company Ordinary Shares from the NYSE.

Following the effectiveness of Form 25, Nielsen intends to file a certification on Form 15 requesting termination of registration of the Company Ordinary Shares and suspension of reporting obligations under the Exchange Act.
